FAQ
Where do I find the TikTok video link?
Open TikTok and find the video you want. Tap the Share button (arrow icon), then select Copy Link. The link is now on your clipboard, ready to paste here.
What link formats are supported?
Standard long URLs: https://www.tiktok.com/@user/video/123456
Short URLs: https://vm.tiktok.com/ZMxxxxxx/
Douyin: https://v.douyin.com/xxxxxx/
Is this free to use?
Yes. KiPiZTIK is completely free, no account required, no download limits.
Does KiPiZTIK store my videos?
No. Videos are served directly from TikTok's servers. KiPiZTIK does not store, cache, or log any video data or download history.
How do I save a video on iPhone?
Tap the Download button, then in Safari choose Download Linked File, or long-press the link and select Save to Files. iOS 13 and above support direct downloads.
Can I download private TikTok videos?
No. KiPiZTIK can only download publicly available videos. Private account videos are not accessible.
