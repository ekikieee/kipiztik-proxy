FAQ
Which platforms are supported?
TikTok (including Douyin), YouTube, and Instagram Reels and posts.
Is this free?
Yes. KiPiZTIK is completely free, no account required, no download limits.
Does KiPiZTIK store my videos?
No. Videos are served directly from the platform's servers. Nothing is stored or logged.
How do I save on iPhone?
Tap Download, then in Safari choose Download Linked File, or long-press and Save to Files. iOS 13+ supported.
Can I download private videos?
No. Only publicly available videos can be downloaded.
